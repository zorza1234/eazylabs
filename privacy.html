Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This privacy policy describes the general approach used by EazyLabs for its apps and website.

Data collection

EazyLabs aims to keep products lightweight and privacy-conscious. Finova's core calculations are designed to work offline and do not require an account for basic calculator functionality.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we may receive the information you choose to include in your message. We use it only to respond to your request and provide support.

Third-party services

Individual apps may use third-party services where necessary for distribution, analytics, crash reporting or other product functions. Any such use will be described in the relevant app's privacy documentation.

Changes

This policy may be updated when our products or practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@eazylabs.app.
